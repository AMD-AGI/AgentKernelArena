# MiniMax call geometry and scoring scope

Exact serving-workload performance is blocked. M=1/M=64 histogram/analytic buckets remain random/replay robustness cases only. They have no captured per-call paging/block controls and cannot be reported as exact HyperLoom performance. The observed oracle cases remain correctness inputs; they are not silently substituted for the declared serving buckets.

All 3 captured correctness cases and the complete tensor inventory remain in [SHAPES.json](SHAPES.json) and [ut/generated_cases.json](ut/generated_cases.json). The performance gate and evidence are recorded in `ut/meta.json:performance_contract`.
